Cookie policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy describes what Sofia Gleda keeps in your browser, and why.

In short

  • Sofia Gleda sets no cookies.
  • We use no visitor statistics, tracking or advertising.
  • Film trailers load from YouTube only if you play them. YouTube may then store its own data in your browser (see section 3).
  • Your browser keeps only what the site needs to work the way you asked: your language, mode, favourite venues, your answer to the cookie notice and, on sofiagleda.com, a copy of the site for offline use.
  • That is why the cookie notice only informs you and has nothing to switch off.

1. Why we do not ask for consent

Consent is required to store information on your device, unless the storage is strictly necessary for a service you have asked for (Article 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC). Your chosen language and mode, the venues you saved as favourites, the record of your cookie choice and the copy of the site that lets it open quickly and work offline are such cases. We keep nothing else.

3. Cookies set by other websites

Some posters load directly from the websites of cinemas and festivals. Those websites may set their own cookies, which we do not control and cannot read. At the date of this policy, the image server of cinemacity.bg (Cloudflare) sets the __cf_bm cookie, which protects against automated traffic and expires after 30 minutes. Their own policies have more information. You can block cookies from other websites in your browser settings, and Sofia Gleda will keep working.

Film trailers come from YouTube. The player loads from www.youtube-nocookie.com (YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode) only when you press the play button, and it is removed when you stop the video or close the film. Until then YouTube and Google receive nothing and store nothing in your browser. Once you play a trailer, YouTube may store information in your browser (cookies or local storage) under its own rules: the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not control and cannot read that data. We do not ask for separate consent for it, because the player loads only at your explicit request: pressing the button. The Privacy policy has more.

4. How to delete the data

You can delete the site's data in your browser settings (for example “Clear site data”). This also deletes your favourite venues and settings, and the cookie notice will be shown again.

5. Cookie settings

The “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of the site opens the notice again. If we ever add optional tools, such as visitor statistics or signing in with an account, they will only run after you explicitly agree, and the notice will ask you again.

More about personal data and your rights is in the Privacy policy.
