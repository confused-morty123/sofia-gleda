Sofia Gleda is run by a private individual, on a non-commercial basis. The site is free, carries no ads and takes no payments. In these terms, “we” means the person who runs the site. The site gives no name or email address; how to reach us is in section 14.

2. What the site offers

Sofia Gleda shows what is on at Sofia's cinemas and theatres: titles, dates, times, halls, prices (where known), descriptions, posters and ratings. We do not sell tickets, take bookings or organise screenings or performances. No registration is needed.

The site is provided as is. We may change its content and features, and we may suspend or close it.

4. Where the information comes from

The listings are collected automatically every day from the websites of the cinemas, theatres and festivals, and for some titles from programata.bg and theatre.art.bg. They are then processed automatically: titles are matched with data from TMDB and OMDb, and texts that exist only in Bulgarian are machine-translated into English with DeepL.

Automatic collection and processing can produce mistakes: a wrong or outdated time, a missing, cancelled or moved screening or performance, or a wrong price, description, poster, rating or translation. Times marked as preliminary have not yet been confirmed by the venue.

5. Check with the organiser

The information published by the cinemas, theatres and organisers prevails. Before you buy a ticket or set off, check the date, time, hall and price on their website or at the box office. Checking is your responsibility.

6. Artificial intelligence

The site was built and is maintained with the help of AI tools. They wrote the code and much of the logic that collects, matches and orders the listings. The listings are published automatically, without a person checking every line. AI can make mistakes, so treat the site as a handy guide, not as an official source.
