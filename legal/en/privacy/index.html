Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy describes what personal data is processed when you use Sofia Gleda, for what purpose, on what legal basis, and what rights you have. It is written in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the “GDPR”) and the Bulgarian Personal Data Protection Act.

In short

  • The site has no user accounts, no forms, no visitor statistics and no ads.
  • We keep no personal data on any server we control. Your settings stay in your browser and are not sent to us.
  • We set no cookies.
  • On every visit, the hosting provider GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes.
  • Posters load directly from TMDB and from the websites of cinemas and festivals, so TMDB and those websites also receive your IP address.
  • Film trailers come from YouTube and load only when you press the play button. Until then nothing is sent to YouTube or Google.
  • To reach us, use the project's public GitHub page. Do not post personal data there.

1. Controller

The controller is the private individual who maintains Sofia Gleda on a non-commercial basis. The site gives no name or email address. The only way to reach us is the project's public GitHub page (see section 8). No data protection officer has been appointed, because Article 37 GDPR does not require one for processing like this.

2. What we do not do

We do not require registration and do not collect names, email addresses or phone numbers through the site. We have no database and keep no personal data on any server we control. We use no analytics, tracking or advertising tools. We do not profile visitors or make automated decisions within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR. We do not sell data or share it for marketing.

3. Visiting the site (hosting)

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a GitHub service. When you open a page, your browser sends GitHub's servers your IP address and technical data such as the browser type and the page address. According to GitHub's documentation, every visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. We have no access to these logs. GitHub processes them under its privacy statement and decides how long to keep them.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the site available and secure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

GitHub, Inc. is based in the United States and is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795).

The fonts are served from our own site too, with no request to any other server.

4. Images from other websites

Film posters and photos load directly from TMDB's servers (image.tmdb.org), and some posters from the websites of cinemas, festivals and programata.bg. Theatre posters load from our own site. To show an image from another website, your browser connects to it and sends your IP address, browser details and the address of our site, without the specific page. Those websites process the data under their own rules. TMDB is run by Xperi Inc. (United States).

Some of these websites set their own cookies. See the Cookie policy for details.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in showing the original posters with the listings (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you prefer not to load them, you can block images from other websites in your browser, for example with a content-blocking extension. The site keeps working and shows its own stand-in posters.

5. Trailers from YouTube

Some films have a trailer on YouTube. Until you press the play button, the site loads nothing from YouTube or Google: you see only the poster, which comes from TMDB (section 4). When you press it, the site embeds YouTube's player in its privacy-enhanced mode (www.youtube-nocookie.com). Your browser then connects to YouTube, and YouTube receives your IP address, browser details and the address of our site. According to Google, in this mode YouTube does not use the viewing to personalise what it shows you, but it may store information in your browser and show non-personalised ads. When you stop the video or close the film, the player is removed from the page.

This site uses YouTube API Services. When you play a trailer, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply. YouTube is run by Google Ireland Limited (Ireland) and Google LLC (United States), which is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in showing you the trailer you asked for by pressing the button (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you prefer no connection to YouTube, simply do not play the trailer. The rest of the site works without it.

6. Links to other websites

When you open a link to a ticket website, a map (Google Maps) or a calendar (Google Calendar, Outlook), your browser connects to it directly and its own rules apply from then on. The Apple Calendar file (.ics) is created in your browser and is not sent to us.

7. What stays in your browser

The site keeps your language, mode (cinema or theatre), favourite cinemas and theatres and your answer to the cookie notice in your browser. On the site's main address (sofiagleda.com), the browser also keeps a copy of the site so that it opens quickly and works offline. None of this is sent to us and we cannot see it. You delete it yourself by clearing the site's data in your browser settings. See the Cookie policy for details.

8. Reaching us through GitHub

You can leave questions and reports on the project's public GitHub page: github.com/confused-morty123/sofia-gleda/issues. You need a GitHub account to post there. Your message is published under that account and anyone can read it. GitHub processes your account and messages under its own terms and privacy statement. When we reply, we use only what you posted there (our legitimate interest in answering questions, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Do not post personal data there: no email address, phone number, home address or other details about yourself or anyone else. If you posted some by mistake, you can edit your message yourself, and we will delete the whole message if you ask.

9. Your rights and how to use them

You have the right to request access to your personal data, its rectification or erasure and the restriction of its processing, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Articles 15, 16, 17, 18 and 21 GDPR). You have the right to data portability (Article 20 GDPR) where processing is based on consent or a contract; there is no such processing at present. If we ever ask for your consent, you will be able to withdraw it at any time.

In practice this means:

  • We keep no personal data on any server we control, so there is nothing of yours with us to give you or to delete.
  • Your settings and favourite venues are only in your browser. You delete them by clearing the site's data in your browser settings (see the Cookie policy).
  • GitHub's visit logs and your GitHub account data are covered by GitHub's privacy statement; send requests about them to GitHub.
  • The data that TMDB and the cinema and theatre websites receive when images load, and YouTube (Google) when you play a trailer, is covered by their own policies; send requests about it to them.
  • If you believe we do process personal data of yours, describe the case at github.com/confused-morty123/sofia-gleda/issues without posting the data itself, and we will answer there. Because we keep no user accounts, we may be unable to link your request to any data (Article 11 GDPR).

10. Complaints

If you believe your rights have been infringed, you can lodge a complaint with the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (Комисия за защита на личните данни): 2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd, 1592 Sofia, Bulgaria, kzld@cpdp.bg, www.cpdp.bg. Under Bulgarian law the complaint must be filed within 6 months of learning of the infringement and no later than two years after it occurred (Article 38 of the Personal Data Protection Act). You can also turn to the supervisory authority of the EU country where you live or work (Article 77 GDPR), or to the courts.

11. Changes

When this policy changes, we publish the new version on this page with its new date. After a significant change, the cookie notice is shown again.
